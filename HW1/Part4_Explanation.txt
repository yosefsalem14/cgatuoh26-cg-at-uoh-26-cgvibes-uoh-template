PART 4: UI Visual Translation

I applied a fixed translation of (+50, +30) pixels to all UI elements
by modifying draw_rect and draw_text. The shift is controlled by the
"UI Shift (click here)" checkbox inside the Widgets window.

WHY CLICKING FAILS:
MicroUI's click detection uses the original coordinates during the UI
logic phase (mu_begin/mu_end), BEFORE rendering. The renderer draws
pixels at (x+50, y+30), but MicroUI checks clicks at (x, y).

WHERE TO CLICK:
To toggle the checkbox, the user must click 50px LEFT and 30px UP
from where the checkbox visually appears (the original logical position).

When shift is OFF, everything works normally.
